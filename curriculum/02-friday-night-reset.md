# Friday Evening Retreat: The Reset (Heart, Identity, and Ownership)

**Setting:** Friday evening (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM). A comfortable, distraction-free room (living room or church lounge) with dinner.  
**Audience:** The 5–8 single men of the pilot cohort.  
**Prerequisite:** Every man brings his completed [Personal 3-T Baseline Dossier](01-light-a-candle-sprints.md).  
**Core Goal:** Transform the participant's fundamental identity from **Anxious Owner / Aimless Consumer** to **Entrusted Steward of the Living God**.  
**Governing ADRs:** [ADR-004](../DECISIONS.md#adr-004--theology-is-author-canon), [ADR-007](../DECISIONS.md#adr-007--sequence-reset-then-plan-then-behavior), [ADR-008](../DECISIONS.md#adr-008--onboarding-post-service-lunch-light-a-candle-sprints-and-earned-self-selection).

---

## Session Schedule (3 Hours)

```
06:00 – 06:45 PM | Shared Dinner & Informal Re-Connection (45 min)
06:45 – 07:15 PM | Unpacking the Candles: What Happened During the Sprints? (30 min)
07:15 – 08:00 PM | The Core Reset: God’s Absolute Ownership & The Steward Identity (45 min)
08:00 – 08:15 PM | Break & Coffee (15 min)
08:15 – 08:50 PM | Cohort Triads: The Dossier Confession & The 3 Big Questions (35 min)
08:50 – 09:00 PM | Commissioning for Saturday & Closing Prayer (10 min)
```

---

## 1. Shared Dinner & Informal Re-Connection (6:00 – 6:45 PM)
- Begin with a generous meal. Hospitality is foundational (from `mr-life-manager` principles).
- Men arrive from a demanding work week. Let them decompress. No formal teaching yet.

---

## 2. Unpacking the Candles (6:45 – 7:15 PM)
*Facilitator prompts the room:*

> "Over the last three weeks, each of you lit some candles. You looked at bank charges, you audited your screens, you paused and prayed at your desks. You did physical behaviors before we even sat down to explain the full theology.
> 
> "Let's go around the circle: **What surprised you most when you compared your PREDICTION to your ACTUAL reality?**"

- **Dynamic:** Keep it conversational. As men share the gap between their predictions and reality (e.g. *"I thought I spent $100 on dining out; it was $420"* or *"I averaged 5 hours a day on my phone"*), nod without judgment. 
- **Facilitator Note:** Praise honesty, not perfection. The gap is the *disorienting dilemma* that prepares the soil of the heart.

---

## 3. The Core Reset: Ownership vs. Stewardship (7:15 – 8:00 PM)
*Theological anchor: [AUTHOR canon, DECISIONS.md draft & source-material]*

### A. The Cultural Illusion: The Myth of Ownership
- Draw a simple diagram on a whiteboard:

```
THE WORLD'S ILLUSION:                   THE KINGDOM REALITY:
┌───────────────────────┐               ┌───────────────────────┐
│     ME = OWNER        │               │     GOD = OWNER       │
│ • My money (I earned it)│             │ • Earth is the Lord's │
│ • My time (it's my life)│             │ • Everything is loaned│
│ • My skills (my talent)│              └───────────┬───────────┘
└───────────┬───────────┘                           │ (entrustment)
            ▼                                       ▼
  Result: Exhaustion,                   ┌───────────────────────┐
  Anxiety, Selfish Drift,               │     ME = STEWARD      │
  Aimless Guilt                         │ • Entrusted Manager   │
                                        │ • Freedom & Purpose   │
                                        └───────────────────────┘
```

### B. The Anchor Scripture
- **Psalm 24:1:** *"The earth is the LORD's, and all it contains, the world, and those who dwell in it."*
- **1 Corinthians 4:7:** *"What do you have that you did not receive? And if you did receive it, why do you boast as if you had not received it?"*
- **Matthew 25:14–15 (The Talents):** The master entrusts *his own* possessions to his servants, each according to his ability.

### C. The Author's Canon: The Reset Statement
*Read clearly and deliberately to the room:*

> *"Everything you have belongs to God. He has loaned it to you and asked you to be a faithful steward of it. That includes your time, your treasure, and your talents. None of it is yours.*
> 
> *"Because this is who you are—a steward, not an owner—you are freed from the crushing burden of having to be the master of your universe. You don't have to defend your empire. You are an entrusted manager in the service of a generous King.*
> 
> *"Our question changes from:*
> - *'How can I get what I want with my time and money?'*
> *to:*
> - *'How does my King want His resources invested for His glory?'"*

### D. The Holy Secular: Vocation as Priesthood
*Connecting to `source-material/secular-work-full-time-service.md`:*
> "You are single men working in the world. You are not 'second-class Christians' waiting to become pastors or missionaries to do real ministry. 
> 
> "Ordinary work is holy. Tomorrow, your desk, your wrench, your spreadsheets, and your customer calls are your altar. As God's mature man in ordinary work, you do your daily tasks wholeheartedly 'as to the Lord.' You are subduing and ruling over the earth in His name. You are not ruled by your wages; your income is converted into kingdom currency."

---

## 4. Cohort Triads: Dossier Confession & 3 Questions (8:15 – 8:50 PM)

Split the men into pairs or triads (2–3 men each). 

### Instructions for Triads:
Every man opens his completed [3-T Baseline Dossier](01-light-a-candle-sprints.md) and answers three questions with his brothers:

1. **The Time Question:** *"If God audited my calendar last week, would He see a life ordered around His kingdom and true rest, or a life consumed by reaction and distraction?"*
2. **The Treasure Question:** *"Where is my money revealing an 'Owner' mentality (hoarding, status, escaping through comfort) rather than a 'Steward' mentality?"*
3. **The Talent Question:** *"Do I wake up Monday morning seeing myself as Christ's representative at my job, or just a guy working for a paycheck?"*

*Ground rule:* **Zero fixing.** The other men listen, nod, and pray. No uninvited advice. Confidentiality is absolute.

---

## 5. Closing & Commissioning for Saturday (8:50 – 9:00 PM)

Reconvene the full circle.

### Facilitator Wrap-Up:
> "Tonight, the foundation was laid. You are not owners. You are God's stewards. 
> 
> "Tomorrow, Saturday, we will build the house. We have three dedicated sessions:
> - **Morning:** Stewarding your **Time** (Building rhythm, margin, and true Sabbath).
> - **Midday:** Stewarding your **Treasure** (Taming the budget, generosity, and kingdom currency).
> - **Afternoon:** Stewarding your **Talents** (Transforming your daily job into holy vocation).
> 
> "We will end tomorrow with each of you walking out with your personal 90-day action plan."

Close in prayer, thanking God for His lavish generosity in entrusting His resources to these men.
Dismiss by 9:00 PM promptly so men rest well for Saturday morning.
