# Post-Service Kickoff Lunch: Facilitator Guide & 3-Door Menu

**Format:** 35-minute informal lunch immediately following Sunday church service.  
**Audience:** Single men (target: 10–15 attending lunch; 5–8 will self-select into the cohort).  
**Cost/Supplies:** Simple lunch (pizza, tacos, or burritos), drinks, printed 1-page "3-Door Menu" handouts, pens.  
**Status:** `[DRAFT]`. The speaking points and the Scripture in Door 3 are drafts for the author to review (ADR-004).  
**Governing ADR:** [ADR-008](../DECISIONS.md#adr-008--onboarding-post-service-lunch-light-a-candle-sprints-and-earned-self-selection).

---

## 1. Facilitator Flow (35 Minutes)

### 00:00 – 00:15 | Food & Casual Fellowship (15 min)
- Keep it light. No formal teaching. Men grab food, sit together, and talk. 
- Break the ice naturally: ask about their week, sports, or work. 
- Let them feel that this is a room of peers, not a lecture hall.

### 00:15 – 00:25 | The 10-Minute Vision Cast (10 min)
*Key tone: Direct, honest, empathetic, zero churchy guilt.*

#### Speaking Points:
1. **The Modern Ache for the Single Man:**
   > "Most of us as single men are caught in an unspoken squeeze. During the week, we give our best energy to our jobs, but by evening and the weekend, we feel adrift. We open our banking app or look at our screen time, and we wonder: *Where did the money go? Where did the hours go? Is my job just a paycheck to pay rent and buy DoorDash until I get married?*"
2. **The World's Lie vs. God's Reality:**
   > "Culture tells you two lies: either grind 80 hours a week to build your own empire, or coast passively and consume entertainment. Scripture gives a completely different reality: **You own nothing. God owns everything.** Your bank account, your calendar, your skills—God has placed them on loan to you right now, as a single man, to steward for His kingdom. Your secular job tomorrow morning is holy worship."
3. **The Problem with Normal Church Programs:**
   > "Most church classes give you 6 weeks of lectures, a fat workbook, and zero lasting change. We don't want to do that. We are running an intensive, in-person weekend retreat in a few weeks, followed by 12 weeks of brother-to-brother support. But there is a catch: **we do not take sign-ups today.**"

### 00:25 – 00:30 | The "No Hostages" Rule & The 3 Doors (5 min)
*Key tone: High agency, inviting, firm.*

#### Speaking Points:
> "We refuse to take hostages. If you are here because someone guilted you, or your parents told you that you 'should' do this, you are free to walk away. Real stewardship requires motivated men who actually want order and fruit in their lives.
> 
> "Instead of signing up, we have a challenge. On the handout in front of you are **Three Doors**: Time, Treasure, and Talent. 
> 
> "Pick the **one** door that is bugging you the most right now. Take 45 minutes between now and Tuesday night at 9:00 PM to do the exercise. It will give you immediate clarity and an undeniable win.
> 
> "If you do the 45 minutes and want to take the full journey, text or email me your completed reflection. The first 6 to 8 men who complete their 45-minute win get the seats at the weekend seminar."

### 00:30 – 00:35 | Handout & Q&A (5 min)
- Distribute the printed handout.
- Answer practical questions (dates of the weekend, location).
- Close with a brief, punchy prayer for clarity and courage.

## 2. Participant Handout: The 3-Door Menu (1-Page Printable)

*(Copy/print this section for each attendee)*

```
================================================================================
                    STEWARDSHIP: LIVING AS GOD'S STEWARDS
                 Choose Your Door: The 45-Minute Quick Win
================================================================================
The Rule: Pick ONE door. Do not try to do all three. Choose the area of your 
life that feels most scattered, noisy, or unmanaged right now.
Complete the 45-minute exercise before Tuesday at 9:00 PM.
================================================================================

[ DOOR 1: TIME ] — "The 168-Hour Reality Check"
Best for: Men struggling with screen-time drift, fatigue, and chaotic weeks.

1. PREDICT (2 min): Before checking your phone, write down:
   - My estimated daily average iPhone/Android screen time: ______ hrs/day.
   - My estimated weekly hours spent on social, streaming, and gaming: ______ hrs.
2. CHECK (10 min): Open your phone Settings → Screen Time / Digital Wellbeing:
   - Actual daily average: ______ hrs/day.
   - Actual hours on entertainment/social apps this past week: ______ hrs.
   - Difference (Actual minus Predicted): ______ hrs.
3. THE 45-MIN WIN (25 min):
   - You have 168 hours this week. Sleep is ~56h. Work + Commute is ~45h. Meals/Basics ~14h.
   - You have roughly 53 unallocated hours. 
   - Right now, pick a block of 90 minutes this week for intentional REST or PRAYER.
   - Put it in your digital calendar right now as a non-negotiable appointment.
4. REFLECTION (8 min): What did the gap between your prediction and reality show you?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ DOOR 2: TREASURE ] — "The 30-Day Spending Leak Finder"
Best for: Men whose money seems to evaporate, leaving little for saving or giving.

1. PREDICT (2 min): Before opening your accounts, write down:
   - What I think I spent last month on eating out, delivery apps, & coffee: $______
   - What I think I have in recurring monthly subscriptions: $______
2. CHECK (15 min): Open your primary banking or credit card app for the last 30 days:
   - Add up every restaurant, DoorDash, UberEats, and coffee charge: $______
   - List every active recurring subscription (streaming, apps, gym, boxes): $______
   - Difference (Actual minus Predicted): $______
3. THE 45-MIN WIN (20 min):
   - Find at least TWO subscriptions or spending habits you can cancel or pause today.
   - Cancel them right now on your phone.
   - Calculated monthly cash reclaimed: $______ per month.
4. REFLECTION (8 min): How does it feel to see the exact dollar amount of your "leakage"?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ DOOR 3: TALENT ] — "The Daily Work Reality Audit"
Best for: Men who feel like their secular job is just a paycheck disconnected from God.

1. PREDICT (2 min): On a scale of 1 to 10:
   - How much do I believe God cares about the specific tasks I do from 9 to 5? [   ]
   - How much of my energy at work is driven by anxiety/pleasing people vs. worship? [   ]
2. CHECK (15 min): Take a blank sheet of paper. Write down your 3 core work tasks.
   - For each task, answer: Who does this actually serve when done with excellence?
   - Name the biggest frustration, temptation, or conflict you face at your desk/job.
3. THE 45-MIN WIN (20 min):
   - Read Colossians 3:23–24: "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord..."
   - Identify ONE concrete, specific action you will do tomorrow at work strictly as
     an offering to God (e.g. encouragement to a co-worker, excellence on an invisible
     deliverable, refusing to complain or gossip).
4. REFLECTION (8 min): What would change if your employer was actually Jesus Christ?

================================================================================
HOW TO CLAIM YOUR SEAT IN THE PILOT COHORT:
When you finish your 45 minutes, text or email your reflection (3-5 sentences)
to [Facilitator Contact]. 
Seats for the retreat cohort are capped at 8 men on a first-completed basis.
================================================================================
```
